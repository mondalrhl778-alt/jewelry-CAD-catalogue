Production blueprint

Reliable geometry first.
Generative polish second.

01

Ingest & normalize

Raster CAD is deskewed and vectorized. Native DXF, STEP and 3DM flow through a CAD renderer to create canonical camera views.

02

Lock the design

Save a geometry manifest: silhouette, stone centres, prongs, hole paths and measured bounds. It becomes the non-negotiable render constraint.

03

Compose & export

Use the generated hero render with deterministic SVG views and a template engine for editable PDFs, SVGs and batch SKU jobs.
